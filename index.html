Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

How long is the free trial?

Your free trial lasts one month. No credit card is required to get started.

How do I get started?

Create your account online and set up Guestlst yourself. If you have questions, email info@guestlst.com.

What does Guestlst cost after the trial?

The Basic plan is CHF 19.90 per month. Optional modules are available for an additional monthly fee, as listed above.

Are there any additional fees?

Prices include taxes. Ticket payment processing is handled through Stripe Connect; applicable Stripe fees depend on your connected account and Stripe's pricing.

What do I need to activate the ticket shop?

Connect a Stripe account in your company settings to enable ticket sales. Ticket categories and shop settings can be managed for each event.
